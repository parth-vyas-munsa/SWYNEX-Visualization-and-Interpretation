Analysis

Basic statistical summaries of gene expression.
#########3box plot what does

The boxplot shows the median, quartiles, spread, and potential outliers in each sample. Similar distributions may indicate comparable expression ranges, but the boxplot alone does not prove differential expression or biological significance.
#############33heat map what does
Similar colors indicate stronger correlation between samples, while different colors indicate weaker correlation. The heatmap helps explore sample relationships but does not prove differential expression or biological significance.
################pca map what does
Samples located close together have more similar expression patterns, while samples farther apart show greater variation. PCA helps identify overall patterns but does not prove statistical significance or causation.
